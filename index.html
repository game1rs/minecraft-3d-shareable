How to Play

BLOCKWILD is an original voxel survival sandbox. No Minecraft code or assets. Explore, mine, craft, farm, and survive.

Controls

  • WASD move, Mouse look, Space jump, Shift sprint, Ctrl crouch
  • Left Click break, hold to mine. Right Click place / use.
  • 1-9 / Wheel hotbar, E inventory, F interact, Q drop, Esc pause
  • F3 debug overlay

First Steps

  • Punch tallgrass for fiber, chop a tree, craft planks → bench (Field Bench).
  • Bench gives 3×3 crafting. Make tools. Stone needs a pick.
  • Kiln smelts ore. Coffer stores 27 stacks. Haulers move items.
  • Till soil with hoe, plant seeds, keep water nearby. Berries and grain fill hunger.
  • Build a bedroll to set spawn and skip night if safe.
  • Find Ember Gate (nightglass frame + flint) and Sky Gate for other dimensions.

Survival

  • Health 20, hunger drains sprint and regen. Eat cooked food.
  • Fall damage, drowning, lava, cactus hurt. Water slows, ice is slick.
  • Creatures: duskhares and antlorns flee, bristleboars neutral, nightlurkers and mirelurkers hostile at night or in caves.
  • Spark system: lever → wire → door/lamp/piston/conveyor. Simulate only active networks.
